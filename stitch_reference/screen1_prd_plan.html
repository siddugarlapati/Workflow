# WorkFlow Product Requirements & Scope ## Overview WorkFlow is an AI-powered task and accountability platform designed for small-to-mid-sized businesses. It moves teams away from scattered spreadsheets and chat messages into a structured, role-based environment with a tamper-resistant audit trail. ## Key Features 1. **Role-Based Dashboards**: Specific views for Managers (oversight) and Employees (execution). 2. **AI Work-Log Verification**: LLM-driven analysis of daily logs to flag vague entries or "bluffing." 3. **Manager "Team Briefing"**: One-click AI summary of team performance, risks, and slippage. 4. **Accountability Engine**: Timestamped logs for every status change and update. 5. **Real-time Task Tracking**: Clear priorities, deadlines, and progress states. ## Design Constraints - **Device**: Desktop (primary). - **Style**: Professional "Gen Z" aesthetic — modern, clean, high-contrast, bold typography, but sophisticated. - **Negative Constraints**: NO neon colors, NO teal blue, NO violet, NO purple, NO gold. - **Tone**: Transparent, efficient, authoritative, and user-friendly. ## Proposed Screen List 1. **Manager Dashboard**: High-level overview of team capacity, task health, and the "Where's my team?" AI Briefing trigger. 2. **Task Control Center (Manager)**: Detailed grid/board for assigning tasks, setting priorities, and viewing the timestamped audit trail. 3. **Employee My-Tasks View**: Focused list of responsibilities with the "Submit Work Log" interface. 4. **AI Verification Report**: Detailed view showing the "Confidence Signal" and AI feedback on a specific employee's work log. 5. **Audit Trail / History**: A dedicated view for managers to see the tamper-resistant log of all organizational changes.
